# Lesson 5.1: Describing People and Things

## Introduction
**Adjectives** (Adjetivos) are words that describe nouns (people, places, and things). They help us give more information and make our conversations more interesting and specific. In this lesson, we will learn essential adjectives to describe physical appearance, personality, and qualities.

## Physical Description (Descripción física)

### Height and Build (Altura y complexión)
*   **Tall** (Alto/a) ↔ **Short** (Bajo/a)
*   **Thin** (Delgado/a) / **Slim** (Esbelto/a)
*   **Overweight** (Con sobrepeso) / **Heavy** (Corpulento/a)
*   **Athletic** (Atlético/a) / **Muscular** (Musculoso/a)

> [!NOTE]
> Avoid using "fat" as it can be offensive. Use "overweight", "heavy", or "large" instead.

### Age (Edad)
*   **Young** (Joven) ↔ **Old** (Viejo/a) / **Elderly** (Anciano/a)
*   **Middle-aged** (De mediana edad)

### Appearance (Apariencia)
*   **Beautiful** (Hermoso/a) - for women and things
*   **Pretty** (Bonita) - for women and things
*   **Handsome** (Guapo) - typically for men
*   **Attractive** (Atractivo/a) - for anyone
*   **Ugly** (Feo/a) - *(Be careful, this is rude!)*

### Hair (Cabello)
*   **Long** (Largo) ↔ **Short** (Corto)
*   **Straight** (Lacio) ↔ **Curly** (Rizado)
*   **Blonde** (Rubio) / **Brown** (Castaño) / **Black** (Negro) / **Red** (Pelirrojo)

### Eyes (Ojos)
*   **Blue eyes** (Ojos azules)
*   **Brown eyes** (Ojos marrones)
*   **Green eyes** (Ojos verdes)

## Personality Adjectives (Adjetivos de personalidad)
*   **Friendly** (Amigable) ↔ **Unfriendly** (Poco amigable)
*   **Nice** (Agradable) / **Kind** (Amable) ↔ **Mean** (Malo/a)
*   **Funny** (Gracioso/a) ↔ **Serious** (Serio/a)
*   **Smart** (Inteligente) / **Intelligent** (Inteligente)
*   **Lazy** (Perezoso/a) ↔ **Hard-working** (Trabajador/a)
*   **Shy** (Tímido/a) ↔ **Outgoing** (Extrovertido/a)
*   **Polite** (Educado/a) ↔ **Rude** (Grosero/a)

## Quality Adjectives (Adjetivos de calidad)

### Size (Tamaño)
*   **Big** (Grande) / **Large** (Grande) ↔ **Small** (Pequeño/a) / **Little** (Pequeño/a)
*   **Huge** (Enorme) / **Tiny** (Diminuto/a)

### Quality (Calidad)
*   **Good** (Bueno/a) ↔ **Bad** (Malo/a)
*   **Excellent** (Excelente) / **Perfect** (Perfecto/a)
*   **Terrible** (Terrible) / **Awful** (Horrible)

### Price (Precio)
*   **Expensive** (Caro/a) ↔ **Cheap** (Barato/a)
*   **Free** (Gratis)

### Difficulty (Dificultad)
*   **Easy** (Fácil) ↔ **Difficult** (Difícil) / **Hard** (Difícil)

### Temperature (Temperatura)
*   **Hot** (Caliente) ↔ **Cold** (Frío/a)
*   **Warm** (Tibio/a) ↔ **Cool** (Fresco/a)

### Speed (Velocidad)
*   **Fast** (Rápido/a) ↔ **Slow** (Lento/a)

### Condition (Condición)
*   **New** (Nuevo/a) ↔ **Old** (Viejo/a)
*   **Clean** (Limpio/a) ↔ **Dirty** (Sucio/a)

## Word Order with Adjectives (Orden de palabras con adjetivos)

### Rule 1: Adjective + Noun
In English, adjectives come **before** the noun:
*   ✅ A **beautiful** house. (Una casa hermosa.)
*   ❌ A house beautiful.

### Rule 2: Be + Adjective
With the verb "to be", adjectives come **after** the verb:
*   ✅ She **is tall**. (Ella es alta.)
*   ✅ The car **is expensive**. (El carro es caro.)

### Rule 3: Multiple Adjectives
When using multiple adjectives, follow this order:
**Opinion → Size → Age → Color → Noun**

*   A **beautiful big old red** car.
*   A **nice small new blue** phone.

## Asking Questions (Hacer preguntas)

### What does he/she look like? (¿Cómo es él/ella físicamente?)
*   **Q:** What does your sister look like?
*   **A:** She is tall and thin. She has long blonde hair and blue eyes.

### What is he/she like? (¿Cómo es su personalidad?)
*   **Q:** What is your teacher like?
*   **A:** She is very friendly and funny.

### How + Adjective (¿Qué tan...?)
*   **How tall** is he? (¿Qué tan alto es?)
*   **How old** are you? (¿Cuántos años tienes?)
*   **How big** is your house? (¿Qué tan grande es tu casa?)

## Common Mistakes (Errores comunes)

### Mistake 1: Adjective after noun
*   ❌ *A car red.*
*   ✅ *A red car.*

### Mistake 2: Using "very much" with adjectives
*   ❌ *She is very much beautiful.*
*   ✅ *She is very beautiful.*

### Mistake 3: Confusing "tall" and "high"
*   ✅ *He is tall.* (for people)
*   ✅ *The building is high.* (for things)
*   ❌ *He is high.*

### Mistake 4: Wrong question form
*   ❌ *How is she look like?*
*   ✅ *What does she look like?*

## Examples (Ejemplos)

**Example 1: Describing a person**
- A: What does your best friend look like?
- B: She is short and slim. She has curly brown hair and brown eyes. She is very pretty.
- A: What is she like?
- B: She is really friendly and funny. She is also very smart.
---
- A: ¿Cómo es tu mejor amiga físicamente?
- B: Es baja y delgada. Tiene cabello castaño rizado y ojos marrones. Es muy bonita.
- A: ¿Cómo es su personalidad?
- B: Es muy amigable y graciosa. También es muy inteligente.

**Example 2: Describing things**
- A: I want to buy a new phone. What's yours like?
- B: It's excellent! It's big and fast. It has a beautiful screen.
- A: Is it expensive?
- B: Yes, it's very expensive, but it's perfect.
---
- A: Quiero comprar un teléfono nuevo. ¿Cómo es el tuyo?
- B: ¡Es excelente! Es grande y rápido. Tiene una pantalla hermosa.
- A: ¿Es caro?
- B: Sí, es muy caro, pero es perfecto.

**Example 3: Describing a place**
- A: How is your new apartment?
- B: It's nice! It's small but clean. The kitchen is new and the bedroom is big.
- A: Is it expensive?
- B: No, it's cheap. Only $500 a month.
---
- A: ¿Cómo es tu nuevo apartamento?
- B: ¡Es agradable! Es pequeño pero limpio. La cocina es nueva y el dormitorio es grande.
- A: ¿Es caro?
- B: No, es barato. Solo $500 al mes.

**Example 4: Meeting someone new**
- A: Tell me about your new boyfriend.
- B: He is tall and handsome. He has short black hair. He is very kind and hard-working.
- A: How old is he?
- B: He is 28 years old.
---
- A: Cuéntame sobre tu nuevo novio.
- B: Es alto y guapo. Tiene cabello negro corto. Es muy amable y trabajador.
- A: ¿Cuántos años tiene?
- B: Tiene 28 años.
