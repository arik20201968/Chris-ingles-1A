# Lesson 1.3: Numbers 1-100 and Time

## Introduction
Numbers are useful for many things: ages, prices, phone numbers, and of course, telling the time.

*   83: Eighty-three
*   97: Ninety-seven

## Important Rules for Numbers
*   Numbers 21-99: Use a hyphen (-) between tens and ones
    *   21: Twenty-one
    *   35: Thirty-five
    *   99: Ninety-nine
*   100: One hundred (not "a hundred" in formal writing)
*   Use "and" in British English: 101 = One hundred and one

## Using Numbers in Real Life

### Ages (Edades)
*   **How old are you?**: ¿Cuántos años tienes?
*   **I am 25 years old**: Tengo 25 años.
*   **She is 30**: Ella tiene 30.

### Prices (Precios)
*   **How much is it?**: ¿Cuánto cuesta?
*   **It's 15 dollars**: Son 15 dólares.
*   **It costs 50 euros**: Cuesta 50 euros.

### Phone Numbers (Números de teléfono)
*   We say each number separately: 555-1234 = "five five five, one two three four"
*   Zero can be said as "zero" or "oh"

## Telling the Time (La Hora)
In English, we usually ask: **"What time is it?"** or **"What's the time?"**

### O'clock (En punto)
*   1:00 -> It's one o'clock.
*   2:00 -> It's two o'clock.
*   12:00 -> It's twelve o'clock (noon/midnight).

### Minutes Past (Después de la hora)
*   2:05 -> It's two oh-five (or five past two).
*   2:10 -> It's two ten (or ten past two).
*   2:15 -> It's two fifteen (or a quarter past two).
*   2:20 -> It's two twenty (or twenty past two).
*   2:30 -> It's two thirty (or half past two).

### Minutes To (Antes de la hora)
*   2:35 -> It's two thirty-five (or twenty-five to three).
*   2:40 -> It's two forty (or twenty to three).
*   2:45 -> It's two forty-five (or a quarter to three).
*   2:50 -> It's two fifty (or ten to three).

### AM and PM
*   **AM** (a.m.): Morning (12:00 midnight to 11:59 AM)
*   **PM** (p.m.): Afternoon/Evening (12:00 noon to 11:59 PM)
*   7:00 AM -> Seven in the morning
*   3:00 PM -> Three in the afternoon
*   9:00 PM -> Nine in the evening

### Time Expressions
*   **In the morning**: Por la mañana (6 AM - 12 PM)
*   **In the afternoon**: Por la tarde (12 PM - 6 PM)
*   **In the evening**: Por la noche (6 PM - 9 PM)
*   **At night**: De noche (9 PM - 6 AM)
*   **At noon**: Al mediodía (12:00 PM)
*   **At midnight**: A medianoche (12:00 AM)

## Real-Life Examples

### Example 1: Asking for age
*   **A**: How old are you?
*   **B**: I'm 22 years old. And you?
*   **A**: I'm 25.

### Example 2: Shopping
*   **Customer**: How much is this book?
*   **Seller**: It's 18 dollars.
*   **Customer**: And this pen?
*   **Seller**: It's 3 dollars.

### Example 3: Asking for the time
*   **A**: Excuse me, what time is it?
*   **B**: It's three thirty.
*   **A**: Thank you!

### Example 4: Making plans
*   **A**: What time is the meeting?
*   **B**: It's at 10:00 AM.
*   **A**: Okay, see you at ten!
