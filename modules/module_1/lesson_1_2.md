## Vowels and Consonants
*   **Vowels**: A, E, I, O, U (and sometimes Y).
*   **Consonants**: All other letters (B, C, D, F, G, H, J, K, L, M, N, P, Q, R, S, T, V, W, X, Y, Z).

## Important Notes
*   **Capital letters** (Mayúsculas): A, B, C, D...
*   **Small letters** (Minúsculas): a, b, c, d...
*   In English, we use capital letters for:
    *   Names: **M**aria, **J**ohn
    *   Countries: **S**pain, **M**exico
    *   Cities: **L**ondon, **N**ew York
    *   Beginning of sentences

## Spelling Examples
*   **Apple**: A-P-P-L-E
*   **English**: E-N-G-L-I-S-H
*   **House**: H-O-U-S-E
*   **Friend**: F-R-I-E-N-D
*   **School**: S-C-H-O-O-L
*   **Computer**: C-O-M-P-U-T-E-R
*   **Family**: F-A-M-I-L-Y

## Common Letter Combinations
*   **TH**: Think, Thank, The
*   **CH**: Chair, Church, Cheese
*   **SH**: Shop, Shoe, Fish
*   **PH**: Phone, Photo (sounds like F)
*   **GH**: Laugh, Enough (can be silent or sound like F)

## Useful Spelling Phrases
*   **How do you spell that?**: ¿Cómo se deletrea eso?
*   **Could you spell your name, please?**: ¿Podrías deletrear tu nombre, por favor?
*   **Could you repeat that, please?**: ¿Podrías repetir eso, por favor?
*   **Slowly, please**: Más despacio, por favor.
*   **Can you spell it?**: ¿Puedes deletrearlo?
*   **Letter by letter**: Letra por letra.

## Real-Life Spelling Situations

### Example 1: At a hotel
*   **Receptionist**: What's your name, please?
*   **You**: My name is Maria Garcia.
*   **Receptionist**: How do you spell your last name?
*   **You**: G-A-R-C-I-A.

### Example 2: On the phone
*   **Person A**: What's your email address?
*   **Person B**: It's john.smith@email.com
*   **Person A**: Can you spell "Smith"?
*   **Person B**: Yes, S-M-I-T-H.

### Example 3: In class
*   **Teacher**: How do you spell "beautiful"?
*   **Student**: B-E-A-U-T-I-F-U-L.
*   **Teacher**: Correct!
