## Important Grammar Rules
*   In English, adjectives (like colors) come **BEFORE** the noun:
    *   ✅ Correct: A **red** car
    *   ❌ Incorrect: A car red
*   Colors don't change for plural:
    *   One red book → Two red books (not "reds books")
*   Use "a" or "an" before singular objects:
    *   **A** book, **An** apple

## Asking About Colors
*   **What color is it?**: ¿De qué color es?
*   **What color is your car?**: ¿De qué color es tu carro?
*   **It's red**: Es rojo.
*   **They are blue**: Son azules.

## Real-Life Examples

### Example 1: Describing objects
*   **A**: What color is your phone?
*   **B**: It's black. And yours?
*   **A**: Mine is white.

### Example 2: Shopping
*   **Customer**: Do you have this shirt in blue?
*   **Seller**: Yes, we have it in blue, red, and green.
*   **Customer**: I want the blue one, please.

### Example 3: In the classroom
*   **Teacher**: What color is the board?
*   **Student**: It's white.
*   **Teacher**: Correct! And the door?
*   **Student**: The door is brown.

### Example 4: Describing your room
*   My room has a **blue** bed, a **white** desk, and a **brown** chair. The walls are **light green**.
