# Lesson 2.3: Possessive Adjectives

## Introduction
Possessive adjectives show that something belongs to someone.

## Table of Possessive Adjectives
| Subject | Possessive Adjective | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I | **My** | This is **my** phone. |
| You | **Your** | Is this **your** car? |
| He | **His** | **His** name is Mario. |
| She | **Her** | **Her** house is big. |
| It | **Its** | The dog likes **its** toy. |
| We | **Our** | **Our** school is small. |
| They | **Their** | **Their** parents are nice. |

## Important Rules

### Rule 1: Possessive Adjectives ALWAYS go before a noun
*   ✅ Correct: **My** book is red.
*   ❌ Incorrect: Book my is red.

### Rule 2: They don't change for plural
*   **My** book (singular)
*   **My** books (plural)
*   **Her** friend (singular)
*   **Her** friends (plural)

### Rule 3: Don't confuse "Its" with "It's"
*   **Its** = possessive (The dog likes **its** toy.)
*   **It's** = It is (contraction) (**It's** cold today.)

### Rule 4: Don't confuse "Your" with "You're"
*   **Your** = possessive (**Your** car is nice.)
*   **You're** = You are (contraction) (**You're** my friend.)

### Rule 5: Don't confuse "Their" with "They're" or "There"
*   **Their** = possessive (**Their** house is big.)
*   **They're** = They are (contraction) (**They're** happy.)
*   **There** = place (The book is over **there**.)

## Possessive Adjectives vs. Possessive Pronouns

### Possessive Adjectives (+ noun)
*   This is **my** book.
*   That is **your** car.
*   **His** phone is new.

### Possessive Pronouns (NO noun)
*   This book is **mine**.
*   That car is **yours**.
*   The phone is **his**.

| Possessive Adjective | Possessive Pronoun |
| :--- | :--- |
| My | Mine |
| Your | Yours |
| His | His |
| Her | Hers |
| Its | (not used) |
| Our | Ours |
| Their | Theirs |

## More Examples
*   I have a dog. **My** dog is small.
*   You have a car. **Your** car is fast.
*   He has a name. **His** name is Peter.
*   She loves **her** family.
*   We like **our** teacher.
*   They clean **their** room.
*   The cat eats **its** food.

## Using Possessive Adjectives with Family
*   **My** mother is a teacher.
*   **Your** brother is tall.
*   **His** sister is 20 years old.
*   **Her** father works in a bank.
*   **Our** parents are kind.
*   **Their** children are students.

## Real-Life Examples

### Example 1: Talking about family
*   **A**: Is this **your** sister?
*   **B**: Yes, **her** name is Ana.
*   **A**: What's **her** job?
*   **B**: She's a doctor.

### Example 2: Describing possessions
*   **A**: I like **your** car!
*   **B**: Thank you! **My** car is new.
*   **A**: What color is **his** car?
*   **B**: **His** car is blue.

### Example 3: At school
*   **Teacher**: Where is **your** homework?
*   **Student**: **My** homework is in **my** bag.
*   **Teacher**: And where is **his** homework?
*   **Student**: **His** homework is on **his** desk.

### Example 4: Talking about pets
*   **A**: What's **your** dog's name?
*   **B**: **Its** name is Max.
*   **A**: Does **your** dog like **its** toys?
*   **B**: Yes, **it** loves **its** toys!
