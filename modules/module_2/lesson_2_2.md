# Lesson 2.2: Articles and Nouns

## Introduction
Nouns are words for people, places, and things. In English, we use articles (**a, an, the**) before nouns.

## Indefinite Articles: A / An
We use **a** or **an** when we talk about something for the first time or something "not specific".
*   Use **A** before a consonant sound: *A book, A car, A house, A university.*
*   Use **An** before a vowel sound: *An apple, An egg, An ice cream, An hour.*

### Important Notes about A/An
*   It's about the **SOUND**, not the letter:
    *   **A** university (sounds like "yuniversity")
    *   **An** hour (the "h" is silent)
    *   **A** European country (sounds like "yuropean")
    *   **An** honest person (the "h" is silent)

## Definite Article: The
We use **The** when the listener knows exactly which thing we are talking about.
*   *The sun, The moon.* (There is only one)
*   *I have a book. **The** book is red.* (We already mentioned it)
*   *The teacher is nice.* (We know which teacher)

### When to use "The"
*   When something is unique: *The sun, The president.*
*   When we mention something again: *I saw a dog. The dog was big.*
*   With superlatives: *The best, The biggest.*
*   With musical instruments: *I play the piano.*
*   With some places: *The bank, The hospital, The cinema.*

### When NOT to use "The"
*   With general plural nouns: *I like dogs.* (not "the dogs")
*   With names of people: *Maria is here.* (not "The Maria")
*   With most countries: *Spain, Mexico.* (except: The USA, The UK)
*   With meals: *I have breakfast at 8.* (not "the breakfast")
*   With languages: *I speak English.* (not "the English")

## Types of Nouns

### Countable Nouns (Contables)
Things you can count: one book, two books, three books
*   **Singular**: a book, an apple, a car
*   **Plural**: books, apples, cars

### Uncountable Nouns (Incontables)
Things you cannot count: water, milk, sugar, money
*   We don't use **a/an** with uncountable nouns
*   We don't make them plural
*   Examples: *water, milk, rice, bread, money, information*

## Plural Rules

### Regular Plurals
*   Most nouns: Add **-s**
    *   book → books
    *   car → cars
    *   table → tables

*   Nouns ending in **-s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x**: Add **-es**
    *   bus → buses
    *   class → classes
    *   dish → dishes
    *   box → boxes

*   Nouns ending in **consonant + y**: Change **y** to **ies**
    *   baby → babies
    *   city → cities
    *   party → parties

*   Nouns ending in **vowel + y**: Add **-s**
    *   boy → boys
    *   day → days
    *   key → keys

*   Nouns ending in **-f or -fe**: Change to **-ves**
    *   knife → knives
    *   wife → wives
    *   leaf → leaves

### Irregular Plurals
*   man → men
*   woman → women
*   child → children
*   person → people
*   tooth → teeth
*   foot → feet
*   mouse → mice
*   fish → fish (same)
*   sheep → sheep (same)

## Real-Life Examples

### Example 1: First mention vs. Second mention
*   **A**: I saw **a** movie yesterday.
*   **B**: Was **the** movie good?
*   **A**: Yes, **the** movie was great!

### Example 2: Using articles in conversation
*   **A**: Do you have **a** pen?
*   **B**: Yes, here is **a** pen.
*   **A**: Thank you! **The** pen is blue.

### Example 3: Countable vs. Uncountable
*   I need **a** book. (countable)
*   I need **some** water. (uncountable)
*   I have **three** apples. (countable)
*   I have **some** money. (uncountable)

### Example 4: Plural forms
*   One child → Two children
*   One person → Many people
*   One box → Three boxes
*   One city → Several cities
